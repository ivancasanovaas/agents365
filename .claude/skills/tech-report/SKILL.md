# tech-report

Required sections (SUBMISSION.md):
1. Approach and why it was chosen.
2. Assets used, and assets tried and dropped.
3. Every labelled training dataset: source, who labelled, size. (None from the provided game.)
4. Possession rules: loose balls, challenges, restarts, dead-ball handling.
5. Limitations and planned improvements.
6. Reproduction steps (exact command that regenerates the CSV).

Also include: external models/datasets/APIs with access links, a table of constants copied from
`PROVENANCE.md`, and a statement of compliance (no hand labels, GT public used only for sanity checks).

Do not include or quote competition data. Keep to a few pages. Name it `<team_name>.pdf` / `.md`.
