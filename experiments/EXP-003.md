# EXP-003: dead-ball (D) detector as extra HMM evidence

STATUS: defined, NOT executed. Written before any result, so the decision rule cannot move afterwards.
Never run on the competition match for model selection; the match gets descriptive aggregates only.

## Hypothesis
`hmm_v0` labels only ~11.5% of graded frames as D (on top of the forced pre-kickoff block). Real stoppages
split possessions, so missing them fuses possessions and lowers spellF1 (fewer, longer spells). Giving the HMM
evidence for "ball not in play" should recover those splits.

## Evidence to add (each variant is cumulative on the previous one)
- A (control): current HMM, team-of-nearest-player evidence, temp 0.3, speed off.
- B: + "ball outside the pitch rectangle" (FIFA Law 9: ball out of play when it wholly crosses a line). Pure
  geometry; the pitch size is the published 105.3 x 68.0 m.
- C: + "ball stationary for a sustained time" (physics: a live ball is rarely at rest). Speed level and minimum
  duration are NOT fixed here: estimated without human labels on EXTERNAL games, recorded in PROVENANCE.md first.
- D: + "last visible ball was near a line, then it vanished" (gap-start proximity to a line), same rule on origins.
- E: + Smart Tagging `SET PIECES` intervals as a soft D/restart prior with a small weight (the interval is a phase,
  not a label; Team = N/A for throw-ins is handled explicitly). Origin of the weight: external analogue or none.
Emissions are learned by counting on external labels (as in `src/model/hmm.py`), never on the match.

## Bench
External games (Metrica Game 3, SkillCorner 1874553, more if added), degraded with seeds 0..N-1 at the published
41% ball loss. Leave-one-game-out for the learned tables.

## Metrics
Primary: spellF1 (headline and mean over IoU). Guards: D-F1, macroF1, fragmentation, number of possessions,
mean absolute boundary latency. All via `possession_metrics.evaluate`, `--bootstrap 1000`.

## Decision rule (fixed in advance)
A variant is kept only if, versus the previous variant, spellF1 improves by more than 0.04 consistently across
seeds AND games, D-F1 does not fall, and macroF1 does not fall by more than 0.02. Otherwise it is dropped and the
simpler variant stays. Negative results are recorded here too.

## Caveats to report
- External D comes from events/phases; the competition D comes from corrected tracking. A gain here supports the
  mechanism, it does not prove the same magnitude on the match.
- Out-of-bounds is the cleanest signal; stationarity can fire on a held ball inside play (goalkeeper), which is
  alive per the possession rules. The bench must include that case or the variant is not trusted.

## Plausibility diagnostics on the match (descriptive, never used to pick a variant)
From `python -m src.profile_datasets --submission ...`: D share, number and duration of H/A runs, H<->A switches,
and how they move across A..E. Absurd values (D near 0% or near 50%, hundreds of one-second spells) mean the
method is wrong, not that a threshold needs nudging.

## Not allowed
Choosing a variant, a speed level or a duration with the 5-minute public GT or by looking at frames of the match.
