# EXP-005: team-shape evidence (compactness asymmetry) for frames without a visible ball

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. Rule below is fixed.

## Hypothesis
The 41% of graded frames without a ball are blind for the nearest-player evidence. Notebook 04 (aggregates only) found
frame clusters where one team is compact (mean pairwise distance ~16 m) and the other stretched (~28-30 m), and the ball
is visible only ~43% of the time in them. A defending team compacts, so the compactness difference between the two teams
should carry information about who has the ball, and it exists on every frame, with or without the ball.

## Evidence added (variant E = C + shape)
- `shape`: bucket of `pair_home - pair_away` (m), mean pairwise distance of each team's visible slots. Edges
  -8, -3, 0, 3, 8 m (6 symbols; ASSUMPTION, coarse, not tuned; PROVENANCE.md). Missing if a team has < 3 visible slots.
- Emission tables are learned by counting on EXTERNAL labelled games, like every other stream. No orientation needed.

## Settings (all external)
Metrica 2-fold by half; Metrica -> SkillCorner; SkillCorner -> Metrica. 3 seeds, calibrated degrader.

## Decision rule (fixed in advance)
Adopt E over C iff, in EVERY setting: mean spellF1 gain > 0.04, no seed regresses, D-F1 does not fall, and macroF1
does not fall by more than 0.02. Otherwise C stays and E is recorded as a negative result.

## Caveats to report
- External team shapes come from other leagues/tracking systems; a gain is evidence for the mechanism, not a guarantee.
- Smart Tagging was not used to choose or validate anything here.

## Results (executed 2026-11-12, 3 seeds)
spellF1 of C -> E (calibrated bench v1 / bench v2 with 16 s ball gaps, see EXP-008):
- Metrica 2-fold: 0.445 -> 0.342 (-0.102) / 0.418 -> 0.338 (-0.080)
- Metrica -> SkillCorner: 0.441 -> 0.411 (-0.030) / 0.446 -> 0.415 (-0.031)
- SkillCorner -> Metrica: 0.432 -> 0.350 (-0.082) / 0.422 -> 0.355 (-0.067)
D-F1 and macroF1 also fall in every setting. **Decision: KEEP C (rule failed in all settings on both benches).**
A direct aggregate check afterwards showed the signal itself is strong (P(home possession | bucket) 0.17 -> 0.82 Metrica,
0.08 -> 0.88 SkillCorner; corr 0.49 / 0.62), so the failure is in how an always-on per-frame naive-Bayes stream interacts
with the alive/dead and boundary decisions (see EXP-006, EXP-007).
