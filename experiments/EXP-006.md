# EXP-006: team-shape evidence that decides only H vs A (neutral for D)

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. Rule below is fixed. Follows a negative result, EXP-005.

## Background
EXP-005 added the compactness asymmetry (`pair_home - pair_away`) as an ordinary HMM stream and was rejected by its rule
(spellF1 -0.03 to -0.10 in all three settings). A direct aggregate check on the labelled external games showed the signal
is strongly informative for possession: P(home possession | bucket) rises from 0.17 to 0.82 (Metrica) and from 0.08 to
0.88 (SkillCorner); correlation with home possession 0.49 and 0.62. The team that has the ball is the more spread one.
Diagnosis: as an ordinary stream it also moves the alive/dead decision, and being present on every frame and highly
autocorrelated it overrides the other evidence.

## Change (single, motivated by the diagnosis)
Stream `shape_ha`: same symbols as `shape`, but its D emission is set equal to the pooled alive emission
(P(sym | D) = P(sym | H or A)), so the stream cannot favour or disfavour D by itself; it only splits H from A.
No other change. Same edges (ASSUMPTION, coarse). Learned by counting on external games.

## Settings and rule (same as EXP-005, fixed in advance)
Three external settings (Metrica 2-fold; Metrica -> SkillCorner; SkillCorner -> Metrica), 3 seeds, calibrated degrader.
Adopt F (C + shape_ha) over C iff in EVERY setting: mean spellF1 gain > 0.04, no seed regresses, D-F1 does not fall,
macroF1 does not fall by more than 0.02.

## Honesty note
This is the second variant of the same idea, chosen after seeing EXP-005 fail; it is justified by a diagnostic that did not
use the decision metric, and the rule is unchanged. If it passes, the gain is reported as one of two attempts.

## Results (executed 2026-11-12, 3 seeds)
spellF1 of C -> F (bench v1 / bench v2):
- Metrica 2-fold: 0.445 -> 0.367 (-0.077) / 0.418 -> 0.365 (-0.053)
- Metrica -> SkillCorner: 0.441 -> 0.435 (-0.006) / 0.446 -> 0.435 (-0.011)
- SkillCorner -> Metrica: 0.432 -> 0.347 (-0.085) / 0.422 -> 0.352 (-0.071)
Making D neutral did not remove the damage (predicted D share still falls, D-F1 down). **Decision: KEEP C.**
