# EXP-007: team-shape evidence only inside ball-missing gaps

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. Rule fixed. Third and LAST attempt of the team-shape idea.

## Background
EXP-005 (shape as an ordinary stream) and EXP-006 (shape neutral for D) were both rejected by the pre-registered rule
(spellF1 down in all settings), although a direct aggregate check shows the compactness asymmetry is strongly informative
for possession in external games (corr 0.49 / 0.62). Diagnosis (to be tested, not assumed): compactness describes the PHASE
of play (tens of seconds) while the possession change is instantaneous, so with the ball visible the slow signal overrides
the fast nearest-player evidence and delays the boundaries, which spellF1 punishes. In ball-missing gaps (median 10.7 s,
41% of graded frames) there is no fast evidence to override.

## Change
Stream `shape_gap`: the `shape_ha` symbol (neutral D emission) but defined only on frames where the ball is NOT visible; it is
absent (-1) when the ball is visible. Everything else as C.

## Settings and rule (same as EXP-005/006)
Three external settings, 3 seeds, calibrated degrader. Adopt iff in EVERY setting: mean spellF1 gain over C > 0.04, no seed
regresses, D-F1 does not fall, macroF1 does not fall by more than 0.02.

## Multiple-attempts note
Three variants of one idea have been tried; requiring the gain in all three independent settings (not just on average)
limits the chance of a lucky pass, and every attempt is recorded here. If this fails, the idea is closed for this match and
a different use (e.g. a post-hoc HMM duration/phase model) must be a new pre-registered experiment.

## Results (executed 2026-11-12, 3 seeds)
spellF1 of C -> G (bench v1 / bench v2 with 16 s ball gaps):
- Metrica 2-fold: 0.445 -> 0.429 (-0.015) / 0.418 -> 0.415 (-0.003)
- Metrica -> SkillCorner: 0.441 -> 0.418 (-0.022) / 0.446 -> 0.426 (-0.020)
- SkillCorner -> Metrica: 0.432 -> 0.424 (-0.008) / 0.422 -> 0.421 (-0.002)
Restricting the stream to ball-missing gaps removes most of the harm but gives no gain, even with realistic 16 s gaps.
**Decision: KEEP C. The idea of team-shape evidence is closed as an HMM stream.** A different use (e.g. an explicit
phase/duration model) would need a new pre-registered experiment.
