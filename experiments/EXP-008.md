# EXP-008: bench v2 (ball-gap length calibrated to the competition profile) and re-run of EXP-003/005/006/007

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. No decision rule is changed.

## Why
The degrader's ball gaps were an ASSUMPTION of 2 s on average (`mean_gap_frames = 50`). The aggregate profile of the
competition tracking shows 150 ball-missing runs in the graded halves: median 10.7 s, p90 33.8 s, p99 104.8 s, mean
about 17 s. A bench whose gaps are 5x shorter cannot show the value (or the harm) of any evidence aimed at gaps, and it
trained and judged every decision so far on gaps where carrying the previous state is almost always right.

## Change (bench only)
`calibrated_params()` now sets `mean_gap_frames = 400` (16 s; a geometric run has median 11.1 s and p90 36.8 s, close to
10.7 s / 33.8 s). The total ball-missing rate stays at the published 41%. Noise (AR(1) 0.999) and glitch (0.7/min)
calibrations are unchanged. The old setting remains available as `calibrated_params(mean_gap_frames=50)` ("bench v1").

## What is re-run on bench v2, with the SAME pre-registered rules
- EXP-003 variants A..D (rule: experiments/EXP-003.md).
- EXP-005 (`shape`), EXP-006 (`shape_ha`), EXP-007 (`shape_gap`) against C (rules in their own files).
Results on bench v1 stay recorded in their files. If a decision flips, the v2 decision applies because v2 is the better
match to the competition data, and the flip is reported as such.

## Not allowed
Choosing the gap length by looking at which value makes a variant win. The value above comes only from profile aggregates.

### Deviation from the literal outcome (disclosed)
The rule compared D with A, never with C, because C had not been kept. Compared directly, D - C is +0.008 spellF1 in Metrica
(noise) and -0.016 in the transfer, with D-F1 -0.203 and macroF1 -0.118 in the transfer; on bench v1 D was also worse than C
(-0.026 / -0.034). The evidence does not show D adding anything over C, and D carries a clear transfer risk. **Decision:
keep C (team + ball-outside + crowd stillness)** as the production variant. This is a judgement call, not the rule's output;
it is recorded here so the technical report can state it. Lesson for later experiments: compare candidates against the
current production variant, not against the last variant kept by a sequential scan.
