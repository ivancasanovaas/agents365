## EXP-010: discriminative emissions (softmax on frame features) with leave-one-game-out on 7 external games

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. Rule fixed. Bench v2 (EXP-008). Candidates compared DIRECTLY with the
production variant C and, for reference, with A.

### Why
Seven open games are now available (Metrica 1, 2, 3; SkillCorner 1874553, 1886347, 1899585, 1925299), so a true
leave-one-game-out (LOGO) is possible for the first time. EXP-005/006/007 showed that a strongly informative signal
(team-shape asymmetry; corr 0.49-0.62 with possession) hurts when added as one more naive-Bayes stream: the product of
per-stream likelihoods ignores that the signals are correlated and that consecutive frames are not independent. A
discriminative model learns the joint effect of all signals at once, so a correlated informative signal is weighted
properly.

### Model (candidate)
- Per-frame feature vector (orientation-free, from tracking only; see src/model/frame_clf.py): ball visibility; per team the
  nearest / 3-nearest / centroid distance to the ball and the counts within 5 and 10 m; ball speed; ball-outside flag and
  outside-run length bucket; crowd-speed buckets; team-shape differences (compactness, spread) and team speeds. Missing values
  are 0 plus an explicit validity flag.
- Home/away symmetry is enforced by augmenting the training set with the team-swapped copy of every frame (H<->A labels
  swapped).
- Multinomial logistic regression (H, A, D), L2 penalty fixed at 1e-2 on standardised features, Adam, 300 full-batch
  iterations, training on every 5th graded frame of the training games. No hyperparameter is tuned.
- HMM integration: emission log-likelihood = temp * (log P(state | x) - log P(state)) per frame (scaled likelihood); the
  transition matrix and the prior come from the same counting fit as C on the same training games; Viterbi inside the graded
  halves as before.

### Candidates and baselines
- `A`: team-only naive Bayes streams (reference).
- `C`: team + ball-outside + crowd naive-Bayes streams (production), temp 0.3.
- `S0.3`: softmax emissions, temp 0.3 (same temperature as C).
- `S0.1`: softmax emissions, temp 0.1 (the softmax posteriors are sharper than the naive-Bayes ones; one alternative value only).

### Protocol
For each of the 7 games as test: train on the other 6 (each degraded with the calibrated bench v2 at seed 999), test on the
held-out game degraded with seeds 3000, 3001, 3002; the per-game score is the mean over the 3 seeds.

### Decision rule (fixed in advance; a candidate vs C)
Adopt a softmax candidate iff ALL hold: (1) the mean spellF1 gain over the 7 held-out games is > 0.04; (2) the gain is > 0 in
at least 5 of the 7 games; (3) mean D-F1 does not fall by more than 0.02; (4) mean macroF1 does not fall by more than 0.02.
If both candidates pass, the one with the higher mean spellF1 gain is adopted.

### Reported but not decisive
C vs A across the 7 games (a stronger check of EXP-003 than the earlier 2-game settings), per-game table, predicted D share
vs true D share, number of segments.

### Not allowed
Using the competition match, its public GT or its profile to choose features, penalties or temperatures. Adding or dropping a
feature after seeing results (a new feature set is a new pre-registered experiment).
