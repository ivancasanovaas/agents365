# EXP-011: temperature of the softmax emissions

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. Rule fixed. Same protocol as EXP-010 (7 external games, leave-one-game-out,
bench v2, 3 test seeds per game). Production reference: `S0.3` (softmax emissions, temp 0.3).

## Why
EXP-010 fixed the temperature at 0.3 (inherited from the naive-Bayes model, not tuned for the softmax) and tried only one
other value, 0.1, which was clearly worse (+0.020 vs C, positive in 4/7). Lower temperature made things worse, so higher
values are the untested direction. The temperature trades the evidence against the transition prior: consecutive frames are
strongly correlated, so a posterior used at full strength (1.0) would over-count evidence, and a small one over-smooths.

## Candidates (all fixed here; no others are tried)
`T0.5`, `T0.7`, `T1.0`: same trained softmax and transitions, only the temperature multiplying
log P(state | x) - log P(state) changes. No retraining.

## Decision rule (candidate vs S0.3 measured in the same run)
Adopt iff: mean spellF1 gain over the 7 held-out games > 0.04; gain > 0 in at least 5 of 7 games; mean D-F1 drop <= 0.02; mean
macroF1 drop <= 0.02. If several pass, the highest mean spellF1 gain wins. Otherwise 0.3 stays.

## Honesty note
Three candidates on one knob. The rule demands the gain in 5 of 7 independent games and a margin of 0.04, which a lucky
value rarely reaches. A smaller but consistent improvement would NOT be adopted by this rule; it is reported anyway.

## Results (executed 2026-11-12, 7 games, 3 seeds, bench v2)
Mean over held-out games (spellF1 / macroF1 / D-F1): T0.3 0.516/0.810/0.834 | T0.5 0.508/0.808/0.832 |
T0.7 0.493/0.807/0.831 | T1.0 0.470/0.806/0.830.
vs T0.3: T0.5 -0.008 (positive in 3/7), T0.7 -0.023 (1/7), T1.0 -0.046 (0/7). All fail. **Decision: KEEP 0.3.**
spellF1 falls monotonically as the temperature rises above 0.3 and also fell at 0.1 (EXP-010): 0.3 sits at or near the optimum.
Macro/D-F1 hardly move with the temperature; only the boundaries (spellF1) do.
