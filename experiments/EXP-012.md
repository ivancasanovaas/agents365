# EXP-012: temporal-context features for the softmax emissions

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. Rule fixed. Same protocol as EXP-010/011 (7 external games, leave-one-game-out,
bench v2, 3 test seeds per game, temp 0.3). Reference: production S0.3 with the 32 base features.

## Why
Ball-missing gaps have a median of 10.7 s in the competition tracking and cover 41% of graded frames; the softmax sees only
the current frame there (team shape, crowd speed), while the information about who had the ball sits before and after the gap.
The HMM transitions carry the previous state, but the strength of the evidence at the edges of the gap and how long ago it
was observed are not available to the emission model. Team-shape and crowd signals also describe a phase of play that lasts
seconds, so their local averages are more reliable than single frames.

## Features added (19; computed from tracking only, offline/non-causal; see src/model/frame_clf.py::context_matrix)
- Window means over +-2 s and +-5 s of: compactness difference, difference of players within 5 m, nearest-team signal, crowd
  speed, speed difference, ball-outside flag, ball-visible share (14 columns).
- Gap context: +-1 s smoothed nearest-team signal; for ball-missing frames the value at the LAST and at the NEXT frame with a
  visible ball and the age of each (log seconds, capped at 60 s) (5 columns).
Home/away swap augmentation applies as before (antisymmetric signals flip sign).

## Variants (all trained on the same 6 games per fold)
- `base`: the 32 base features (= production S0.3, re-trained in this run as the reference).
- `+win`: base + the 14 window columns (ablation, not decisive).
- `+gap`: base + the 5 gap columns (ablation, not decisive).
- `ctx`: base + all 19 columns (the candidate).

## Decision rule (ctx vs base)
Adopt `ctx` iff: mean spellF1 gain over the 7 held-out games > 0.04; gain > 0 in at least 5 of 7 games; mean D-F1 drop <= 0.02;
mean macroF1 drop <= 0.02. The ablations are reported for the technical report only; if `ctx` fails but an ablation passes,
that ablation is NOT adopted by this experiment (it would need its own pre-registered run).

## Not allowed
Adding, removing or rescaling features after seeing the results; using the competition match, its profile or its output to choose
among variants. (Aggregates of the tracking may MOTIVATE a hypothesis, as the ball-gap lengths did here; they never select a variant.)

## Results (executed 2026-11-12, 7 games, 3 seeds, bench v2)
Mean over held-out games (spellF1 / macroF1 / D-F1): base 0.516/0.810/0.834 | +win 0.519/0.809/0.825 |
+gap 0.521/0.812/0.834 | **ctx 0.526/0.814/0.834**.
ctx vs base: mean dSpell **+0.010** (positive in 5/7), dD-F1 -0.001, dMacro +0.005 -> below the 0.04 margin -> **fail**.
Ablations (not decisive): +win +0.003 (5/7, D-F1 -0.009), +gap +0.005 (5/7). **Decision: KEEP base (32 features).**
Reading: the gain exists in 5 of 7 games and is small; the HMM transition prior already carries the state through gaps, so
extra long-range evidence adds little. Not adopted by the pre-registered rule.
