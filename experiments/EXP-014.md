# EXP-014: untouched hold-out games (unbiased estimate) and confirmation of the MLP

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE the hold-out games were loaded or scored. Rule fixed.

## Why
All seven external games were used repeatedly in model decisions (EXP-003 to EXP-013), so the leave-one-game-out numbers are
optimistic. Four further SkillCorner A-League matches (ids 1927964, 1953632, 1959846, 1986691; pinned to the same source commit)
are held out: they are NOT in `registry.available()`, are never used for training, tuning or selection, and are scored once here.
EXP-013 left an open question: the MLP beat the softmax in 7 of 7 games (+0.027 spellF1) but below the 0.04 margin.

## Protocol
- Models are trained exactly as in `src/fit_params.py` on the 7 development games (degraded, seed 999): the naive-Bayes variants A
  and C, the production softmax S, and the MLP of EXP-013 (same hyperparameters, no change).
- Each hold-out game is degraded with the calibrated bench (seeds 3000, 3001, 3002) and decoded with temp 0.3; the per-game score
  is the mean over the 3 seeds. Same scorer (`possession_metrics.evaluate`).

## Reported (not decisive)
spellF1 / macroF1 / D-F1 of A, C, S and the MLP on each hold-out game and on average, predicted D share versus true D share.
This is the unbiased estimate of the production model against the earlier variants. The organiser baseline is not computed here
(it needs the competition format); on the competition match smooth50 scores 0.243.

## Decision rule for the MLP (confirmation of a directional hypothesis suggested by EXP-013)
Adopt the MLP in place of the linear softmax iff: its spellF1 is higher than the softmax's in at least 3 of the 4 hold-out games,
the mean gain is at least +0.02, and the mean D-F1 and mean macroF1 do not fall by more than 0.02. Origin of the +0.02 margin: half
of the 0.04 noise band, justified because this is a confirmation on fresh games of a hypothesis already supported by a 7/7 sign
pattern, not a search; chosen before any hold-out data was scored. Otherwise the linear softmax stays.
If the MLP is adopted, production switches to it (`models/` refit with the MLP on all 7 development games, no use of the hold-out).

## Not allowed
Using the hold-out games for any choice other than this rule: no new features, temperatures, penalties or architectures are
selected on them. After this experiment they are retired as a test set; further changes would need new fresh games.
