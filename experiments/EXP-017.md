# EXP-017: train the emissions also on the DFL games (native labels)

STATUS: defined 2026-11-12 BEFORE running. Rule fixed. Bench v2, 3 test seeds per game, temp 0.3, 32 base features, MLP emissions
(the production recipe). The 4 SkillCorner hold-out games stay retired and are not used. After EXP-016 the 7 DFL games are
development games.

## Why
EXP-016 (fresh evaluation) showed that models trained only on event/phase-derived labels keep their ranking on a different provider
and league with native per-frame labels (D-F1 0.855, alive/dead F1 0.873, team accuracy 0.708 for the MLP), but they predict
3-9 points more dead ball than the native D (predicted 0.42-0.59 vs true 0.41-0.52 in 6 of 7 matches). The competition's ground
truth is defined from corrected tracking ("D where the ball is dead, otherwise the team the tracking names as owning it"), i.e.
like the DFL's native `BallStatus` / `BallPossession` and unlike our event-derived external labels. Training on native labels may
move the dead-ball boundary to the competition's definition. The DFL possession flips are about twice as frequent as the
competition's public ground truth (aggregate fact), so training on them may also encourage fragmentation: the emissions are
frame-level, and the transition matrix and prior stay estimated on the 7 original games (coarser labels, mean spell ~12 s).

## Variants (the same MLP, the same hyperparameters)
- `dev`: emissions trained on the original development games only (production today).
- `dev+DFL`: emissions trained on the original development games and the DFL games (every 5th graded frame plus the team-swapped
  copy of each game). Transitions and initial prior are identical in both variants: counted on the original development games.

## Protocol
- DFL folds (7): hold out one DFL game; `dev` trains on the 7 development games; `dev+DFL` on the 7 development games plus the other
  6 DFL games.
- Development folds (7): hold out one original game; `dev` trains on the other 6 original games (as in EXP-013);
  `dev+DFL` adds all 7 DFL games.
- Metrics: spellF1, macroF1, D-F1, alive/dead F1, strict team accuracy on true H/A frames, predicted D share versus true D share,
  and (reported only) spellF1 against DFL labels with possession spells shorter than 3 s merged.

## Decision rule (adopt `dev+DFL` iff ALL hold)
1. DFL folds: mean D-F1 not lower than `dev` by more than 0.005; mean |predicted D share - true D share| not larger than with `dev`;
   mean team accuracy not lower by more than 0.005.
2. Development folds (the no-harm check on event-derived, coarser labels): mean spellF1 not lower than `dev` by more than 0.01;
   mean macroF1 and mean D-F1 each not lower by more than 0.02.
If adopted, production is retrained on all 14 games with this recipe and `models/` regenerated; otherwise it stays as is.

## Caveat to report
The DFL folds measure agreement with the DFL labels, so condition 1 is expected to hold; the argument that it helps on the
competition match rests on the identical label semantics (native ball status and owning team), not on a measurement on the
competition. Condition 2 is the real test: it must not damage the coarser possession labels.

## Not allowed
Using the competition match, its public GT or its output; changing the 3 s sensitivity or the recipe after seeing results.
